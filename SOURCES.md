# Sources

*Reference: the research behind this plugin. Each entry: title, link, what it informed.*

## Occupational data and job postings

- **Historians, O*NET 19-3093.00 (My Next Move summary)** - https://www.mynextmove.org/profile/summary/19-3093.00 - collecting information from archives, records, diaries, photographs and publications; judging whether material is genuine and how important; writing or reviewing publications and exhibits for accuracy (agent mission, `historical-research`, `source-criticism`, `fact-check`).
- **O*NET task list, Historians** - https://www.onetonline.org/search/task/compare/19-3093.00?d=3729 - the full task list behind the summary (agent responsibilities).
- **Historian GS-12, Department of the Air Force, USAJOBS** - https://www.usajobs.gov/job/697882400 - researching and writing an organisation's history from documents and key personnel; serving as authoritative advisor and answering queries; planning complex studies; evaluating source validity; oral history (agent, explanation).
- **Historian GS-7/9, National Park Service, USAJOBS** - https://www.usajobs.gov/job/807558000 - the developmental level of the career ladder: literature review, fieldwork, consultations with Native Americans, writing text and illustrations for projects led by senior historians, answering public inquiries (career levels, `heritage-ethics`).
- **Historian or archaeologist research consultant for a history game (The Hub)** - https://thehub.io/jobs/67cea7942b88ff09435144b4 - researching a game's historical features and illustrations and working with design, illustration and graphic design (agent, `history-for-design`, `reference-set`).
- **Historians as consultants and contractors, American Historical Association** - https://www.historians.org/resource/careers-for-students-of-history-historians-as-consultants-and-contractors/ - clients (agencies, companies, cultural institutions, media producers), deliverables on time, exhibit scripts and label text, historical integrity in media work (explanation, workflow outputs).

## Professional bodies and standards

- **Statement on Standards of Professional Conduct, American Historical Association** - https://historians.org/resource/statement-on-standards-of-professional-conduct - never fabricating or suppressing evidence, reporting evidence against one's view, citation as a trail others can follow, being "explicit, thorough, and generous" in acknowledging debts, plagiarism, multiple perspectives, public history without distortion (agent "Never" list, `citation-and-evidence`, `source-criticism`).
- **History Discipline Core, AHA Tuning Project** - https://www.historians.org/resource/history-discipline-core/ - history as interpretation built from surviving evidence; gathering, questioning and synthesising complex material; judging sources for credibility, position and perspective; conflicting evidence; open-ended questions and research strategies; narratives for different audiences (`historical-research`, `source-criticism`, explanation).
- **NCPH Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct, National Council on Public History** - https://ncph.org/about/governance-committees/code-of-ethics-and-professional-conduct/ - integrity, cultural inclusiveness, respecting client decisions while keeping independent judgement, declining work beyond competence, disclosing conflicts (agent boundaries, `heritage-ethics`, `history-for-design`).
- **OHA Principles and Best Practices, Oral History Association** - https://oralhistory.org/?p=8350 - the profession's principles for recording oral history, with a statement of narrators' rights, for oral tradition recorded with consent (`historical-research`, `heritage-ethics`).
- **Conference on Latin American History, AHA affiliated society** - https://www.historians.org/affiliated-society/conference-on-latin-american-history - the professional body for Latin American history, its mission of diffusing knowledge about the region (`latin-american-history`).
- **Hispanic American Historical Review, Duke University Press** - https://www.dukeupress.edu/Hispanic-American-Historical-Review - a leading journal of Latin American history (`latin-american-history`).

## Latin American sources and finding aids

- **Digital Florentine Codex, Getty Research Institute** - https://www.getty.edu/news/a-rare-500-year-old-manuscript-gets-a-second-life-online - the digital edition at florentinecodex.getty.edu: Nahuatl and Spanish transcriptions, English and Spanish translations, about 2,500 searchable images (`latin-american-history`, `visual-and-material-reference`).
- **Codex Mendoza digital edition, King's College London** - https://www.kcl.ac.uk/archive/news/ddh/2015/codex-mendoza - a digital edition of a key sixteenth-century source made with INAH and the Bodleian Library (`latin-american-history`).
- **Handbook of Latin American Studies (HLAS Online)** - https://library.udel.edu/databases/hlas/ - the Library of Congress's annotated bibliography for finding scholarship on the region (`latin-american-history`, `historical-research`).
- **FAMSI research department** - https://www.famsi.org/research/ - Mesoamerican research resources, including codices (`latin-american-history`).

## Heritage, representation and rights

- **United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples, article 31** - https://www.vcc.ca/media/vancouver-community-college/content-assets/documents/policies/appendices/510-Appendix-A-UNDRIP-Article-31.pdf - Indigenous peoples' right to maintain, control, protect and develop their cultural heritage, traditional knowledge and cultural expressions (`heritage-ethics`).
- **CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance** - https://portal.lincsproject.ca/docs/terms/care-principles-for-indigenous-data-governance - collective benefit, authority to control, responsibility, ethics (`heritage-ethics`).
- **Never Alone (Kisima Ingitchuna), Museum of Design Atlanta** - https://www.museumofdesign.org/full-circle-never-alone - a game made with an Indigenous community, its elders and a cultural ambassador, as an example of consultation and credit (`heritage-ethics`, explanation).
- **The Met Open Access image policy** - https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources - CC0 for images of works the holder believes are public domain, the Open Access mark, and licensing for everything else (`visual-and-material-reference`).

## History in games and media

- **Assassin's Creed's resident historian on mixing fact with fiction (History on the Net)** - https://www.historyonthenet.com/120-assassins-creeds-resident-historian-maxime-durand-mixing-fact-fiction - an in-house historian keeping story and play accurate to the period while the team takes creative liberties (`history-for-design`, explanation).
- **Digital Games as History, Adam Chapman (Routledge)** - https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/9781138841628 - how videogames represent the past and offer access to historical practice; accuracy versus authenticity (`history-for-design`).
