*How-to guide, for someone on a product team who has chosen to depart from history and needs it recorded.*

# How to record a deliberate departure

A departure is a choice to show something history does not support:
for play, clarity, scope or tone, or because the record is silent.
Recorded, it is a design decision. Unrecorded, it reads as an error.

## Steps

1. Find the project's departures record (the profile names its place;
   if it has none, create it as a reference page next to the research
   briefs).
2. Add one row with:

   | Field | Write |
   |---|---|
   | What the product does | One line |
   | What history says | With citation and confidence, from the brief or fact check |
   | Why it departs | Play, clarity, scope, tone, or a gap in the record |
   | Who decided and when | The role or person, and the date |
   | How it is signalled | Credits, an in-product note, a site page, or not at all, and why |

3. If the departure touches how a living people is shown, run the
   heritage checks in the `heritage-ethics` skill first; a departure
   never excuses a stereotype or the use of sacred material.
4. Commit it with the work it explains, as a `docs:` change.
