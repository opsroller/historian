# history-for-design

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Skill |
| File | [`skills/history-for-design/SKILL.md`](../../skills/history-for-design/SKILL.md) |
| Loaded when | research must shape what a team builds or be checked against it - turning findings into design, art, writing, audio and UI constraints and opportunities; weighing accuracy against authenticity and play; keeping the record of deliberate departures from history; checking a design, script, asset, scene or page for errors, anachronisms and misrepresentation; and writing timelines, glossaries and naming guides the whole team uses. |
| Referenced by | [research-brief](workflow-research-brief.md), [reference-set](workflow-reference-set.md), [fact-check](workflow-fact-check.md) |

## Inputs

Research findings, or a design, script, asset, scene, UI text or page to check.

## Outputs

Briefs with per-role direction, the departures record, timelines and glossaries, and checks with verdicts and fixes.

## At a glance

| Step | Does |
|---|---|
| Accuracy and authenticity | Both are needed; simplify without misrepresenting; invent only as a recorded choice |
| Research into direction | Answer, must, opportunities, free to invent, avoid, per role |
| Departures record | What the product does, what history says, why, who decided, how signalled |
| Checking | Verdicts: supported, recorded departure, error, anachronism, unsupported, representation; severity by harm |
| Shared references | Timeline, glossary and naming guide, reference sets |

## Quality bar

- Every brief tells each role what it means for them.
- Every departure is in the record with who decided it.
- Names are consistent with the glossary everywhere.

## Documentation owed

Briefs, the departures record, the timeline, the glossary and fact checks as reference; the product's approach to the past as an explanation. Written in the same change as the work, one Diataxis kind per page.
