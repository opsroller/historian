# heritage-ethics

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Skill |
| File | [`skills/heritage-ethics/SKILL.md`](../../skills/heritage-ethics/SKILL.md) |
| Loaded when | whenever a product depicts real peoples, faiths or cultures, past or present - checking for stereotype, caricature and exoticism; identifying descendant and living communities and their sacred, restricted or funerary material; using names, languages and terms respectfully; deciding when consultation or permission is owed and raising it early; crediting knowledge holders; and keeping advocacy or marketing from distorting the record. |
| Referenced by | [research-brief](workflow-research-brief.md), [reference-set](workflow-reference-set.md), [fact-check](workflow-fact-check.md) |

## Inputs

Any depiction of a real people, faith or culture, past or present.

## Outputs

Heritage findings with fixes, consultation needs raised to the owner, and credits.

## At a glance

| Step | Does |
|---|---|
| Principles | Integrity, inclusiveness, rights over heritage (UNDRIP article 31), the CARE principles |
| Checks | Stereotype, flattening, erasure, sacred and restricted material, names and language, who benefits, framing |
| Consultation | When it is owed, whom to ask, raised early to the owner, never claimed when not given |
| Credit | Knowledge holders, translators, consultants and scholars; disclosed funding or access |

## Quality bar

- Every depiction of a people has passed the checks, with findings recorded.
- Sacred, restricted and funerary material is left out or used with recorded permission.
- Consultation needs are raised before the work they affect starts.

## Documentation owed

Heritage findings in the fact check or brief; the product's approach to representation as an explanation page; consultation requests and decisions in the decision record. Written in the same change as the work, one Diataxis kind per page.
